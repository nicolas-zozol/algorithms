Greedy algorithms
----

Take always the best local optimum and that MUST give us the best global optimum. It's not always working. 
For knapsack-greedy, we want to add knap by better ratio of value/weight instead of finding all combinations.



Recursivity
====


Pro : fit well to some problems - not all.
Con: often slow due to overlaps


Dynamic algorithms
-----


Dynamic programming is a technique for solving problems of recursive nature, **iteratively** and is applicable when the computations of the subproblems overlap
-> Able to transform a recursion with an iteration. 
Usually by tabulation, but may use memoization.
